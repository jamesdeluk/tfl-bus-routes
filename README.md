# TfL bus routes

An interactive Streamlit map for exploring London bus routes and their published stops.

## Pages

### Show

Enter one or more route numbers to display their published route geometry and stops on the map.

### Explore

Place a pin to show routes with a published stop within a chosen distance. Routes are shown in
matching map colours, with an optional night-routes-only view.

### Go

Place Pin A and Pin B to compare the nearest routes at two locations. By default, only routes
selected for both pins are displayed. Optional controls reveal nearby-stop and exact shared-stop
overlaps.

### Statistics

View scheduled inter-stop times, stop density, spacing, and equivalent walking times for each
route direction.

### Spacing

Explore the distribution of straight-line distances between consecutive stops. Select routes and
switch the histogram between distance or walking time, and between counts or percentages.
